Pogradec & Lake Ohrid

One of Europe's oldest lakes, on Albania's quiet side

Best time
June–September
Time to spend
1–2 days
Region
The South-East
Coordinates
40.90° N 20.65° E

Lake Ohrid is one of the oldest and deepest lakes in Europe, shared between Albania and North Macedonia and protected as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Pogradec is the main town on the Albanian shore.

Life here is slow and lakeside: swimming from small beaches, fish dinners by the water, and the clear springs at Drilon where the lake is fed.

Don't miss in Pogradec & Lake Ohrid

  • The springs and gardens at Drilon
  • Lakeside villages of Tushemisht and Lin
  • Swimming in the clear lake water in summer
  • Fresh lake fish at a waterside restaurant

How to get to Pogradec & Lake Ohrid

  • From Tirana: about 3 hours by bus or car.
  • From Korçë: about 45 minutes.

Watch out for

  • The famous Ohrid trout is protected; ask what fish you're being served.
  • Accommodation is limited outside summer.
