Valbona

Alpine meadows, glacial river, enormous skies

Best time
June to September
Time to spend
2–3 days
Region
The North
Coordinates
42.45° N 19.89° E

Valbona Valley National Park is a broad, wild valley carved by the Valbona river, with jagged peaks on every side. It's quieter than Theth and a wonderful place to slow down for a few days.

Most visitors arrive by the Lake Koman ferry and leave on foot over the pass to Theth, a route that has become a highlight of any trip to Albania.

Don't miss in Valbona

  • Hike Valbona → Theth over the Valbona Pass (about 1,800 m)
  • Swim in (very cold) river pools along the valley
  • Day hikes to the Kukaj lakes or Rragam
  • Guesthouse dinners with local cheese, honey and raki

How to get to Valbona

  • Via the Lake Koman ferry, then minibus from Fierzë: a full but spectacular day from Shkodër.
  • By road via Bajram Curri, which connects to Kosovo and Tirana.

Watch out for

  • Many guesthouses offer to send luggage around by car while you hike to Theth. Arrange it the evening before.
  • Start the hike early: the climb is steep and exposed to sun.
  • Cash only in most places.
