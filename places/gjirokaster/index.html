History · The South

Gjirokastër

The City of Stone, under a giant fortress

Best time
April–June and September–October
Time to spend
1–2 days
Region
The South
Coordinates
40.08° N 20.14° E

Gjirokastër's Ottoman-era tower houses, roofed in grey stone slabs, climb the slopes below one of the largest castles in the Balkans. The old town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and feels unlike anywhere else in Albania.

It's also the birthplace of novelist Ismail Kadare, whose childhood home is now a museum, and of communist leader Enver Hoxha.

Don't miss in Gjirokastër

  • Gjirokastër Castle and its views over the Drino valley
  • Restored tower houses such as Skenduli and Zekate
  • The old bazaar's stone lanes and craft shops
  • The Cold War tunnel under the castle hill

How to get to Gjirokastër

  • From Tirana: buses take around 3.5–4 hours.
  • From Sarandë: about 1–1.5 hours by bus or car, passing the Blue Eye.
  • Buses stop in the new town at the bottom of the hill. A short taxi ride up saves a steep climb with luggage.

Watch out for

  • The polished stone streets are very slippery, especially when wet. Wear shoes with grip.
  • Parking in the old town is tight; leave the car lower down if you can.
