History · Central Albania

Krujë

Skanderbeg's castle and a cobbled old bazaar

Best time
All year
Time to spend
Half a day
Region
Central Albania
Coordinates
41.51° N 19.79° E

Krujë clings to a mountainside north of Tirana, and its castle was the stronghold of Skanderbeg, Albania's national hero, who held off Ottoman armies here in the 15th century.

A restored Ottoman bazaar leads up to the castle, lined with shops selling carpets, felt hats and antiques. It's an easy half-day trip from Tirana and very close to the airport, so it also works on your first or last day.

Don't miss in Krujë

  • The Skanderbeg Museum inside the castle walls
  • The Ethnographic Museum in a traditional Ottoman-era house
  • Browsing the old bazaar
  • Views over the plain to the coast

How to get to Krujë

  • From Tirana: about 1 hour by car or bus.
  • From Tirana airport: around 30–40 minutes by car.

Watch out for

  • The bazaar's cobbles are steep and slippery.
  • Museums are usually closed one day a week; check before you go.
  • Bargain politely in the bazaar.
