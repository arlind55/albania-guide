History · Central Albania

Durrës

Roman ruins and a long sandy shore, near Tirana

Best time
May–June and September
Time to spend
1 day
Region
Central Albania
Coordinates
41.31° N 19.45° E

Durrës is one of the oldest cities on the Adriatic, founded by Greek colonists in the 7th century BC. In the middle of town sits a huge Roman amphitheatre, rediscovered in the 1960s and still being excavated.

It's also Albania's main port and the closest beach to Tirana, with a long seafront promenade and sandy beaches stretching south.

Don't miss in Durrës

  • The Roman amphitheatre and its early Christian chapel mosaics
  • The Archaeological Museum on the seafront
  • The Venetian tower and old city walls
  • An evening walk on the promenade

How to get to Durrës

  • From Tirana: about 40 minutes by car or bus.
  • Ferries arrive here from Italian ports such as Bari.

Watch out for

  • Town beaches get very crowded in summer; quieter sand is further south.
  • Traffic on the Tirana–Durrës road is heavy on summer weekends.
