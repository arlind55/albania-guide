Mountains · The North

Theth

A remote village deep in the Accursed Mountains

Best time
June to September (the pass is snowbound outside this window)
Time to spend
2–3 days
Region
The North
Coordinates
42.40° N 19.77° E

Theth is a scattering of stone houses and guesthouses in a high valley ringed by the limestone peaks of the Albanian Alps, known locally as the Accursed Mountains. Family guesthouses serve home-cooked dinners made from what they grow.

From here you can hike to waterfalls and Theth's own Blue Eye pool, or cross the pass to Valbona on one of the Balkans' best day hikes.

Don't miss in Theth

  • Hike Theth → Valbona over the Valbona Pass (about 6–8 hours)
  • Grunas waterfall and the Theth Blue Eye pool
  • The Lock-in Tower, a refuge from the blood-feud era
  • Theth's lone church against the mountains

How to get to Theth

  • From Shkodër: the road is now paved; minibuses take about 2.5–3 hours, leaving early morning.
  • On foot from Valbona: the classic one-day crossing over the pass.

Watch out for

  • There are no ATMs in Theth. Bring enough cash in lek for guesthouses, food and transport.
  • Mobile signal is patchy. Download offline maps before you go.
  • Mountain weather changes fast. Carry layers, water and a headtorch on longer hikes.
