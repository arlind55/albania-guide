Nature · Central Albania

Osum Canyon

A river carved deep into the limestone

Best time
March–May for rafting; summer for walking and swimming
Time to spend
1 day
Region
Central Albania
Coordinates
40.48° N 20.21° E

Southeast of Berat, the Osum river has cut a canyon about 13 km long through the limestone near Çorovodë, with sheer walls, waterfalls and caves.

In spring, when the river is high, rafting trips run through the gorge. In summer the water drops and you can walk and swim in parts of it, or look down from viewpoints along the rim road.

Don't miss in Osum Canyon

  • Rafting through the canyon in spring
  • Viewpoints along the canyon rim
  • Swimming in the river in summer
  • Combining it with a night in Berat

How to get to Osum Canyon

  • From Berat: about 1.5–2 hours by car to Çorovodë.
  • Most visitors come on a guided day trip or rafting tour from Berat.

Watch out for

  • Rafting depends on water levels; trips usually stop by early summer.
  • The road is slow and winding; allow plenty of time.
  • Facilities are basic; bring water and snacks.
