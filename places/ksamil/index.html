Beaches · The South

Ksamil & Butrint

Turquoise coves and a 2,500-year-old city on the same afternoon

Best time
June and September (July–August is very crowded)
Time to spend
2–3 days
Region
The South
Coordinates
39.77° N 20.00° E

Ksamil is the postcard Albania most people picture: shallow, impossibly clear water and three small wooded islets you can swim or paddle out to. It sits at the very southern tip of the Albanian Riviera, a short hop from Sarandë and within sight of Corfu.

Ten minutes further south is Butrint, a UNESCO World Heritage Site where Greek, Roman, Byzantine and Venetian ruins sit in a forest beside a lagoon. Go early or late in the day, when the light is soft and the tour groups have gone.

Don't miss in Ksamil & Butrint

  • Swim or kayak out to the Ksamil islets
  • Walk the Greek theatre, baptistery and Venetian tower at Butrint
  • Mussel farms and seafood lunches on Lake Butrint
  • Sunset over Corfu from the Sarandë promenade

How to get to Ksamil & Butrint

  • From Corfu: fast ferries to Sarandë take around 30–40 minutes, then 20 minutes by bus or taxi to Ksamil. This is the easiest way in for many first-time visitors.
  • From Tirana: buses to Sarandë take roughly 5–6 hours. Local buses run Sarandë → Ksamil → Butrint throughout the day in summer.
  • By car: the coastal road via Vlorë and the Llogara Pass is beautiful but slow; the inland route via Gjirokastër is faster.

Watch out for

  • Most of the beach is covered with paid sunbeds in summer. A day bed is normal; free patches fill up early.
  • July and August bring heavy traffic and prices to match. Book stays well ahead.
  • Build quality and noise vary a lot between apartments; read recent reviews.
