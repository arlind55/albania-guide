The Blue Eye

A bottomless spring of impossible blue

Best time
Any time of year; early morning to beat the crowds
Time to spend
Half a day
Region
The South
Coordinates
39.92° N 20.19° E

Syri i Kaltër, the Blue Eye, is a karst spring where water wells up from a depth divers have never fully reached. The centre glows deep blue, fading to turquoise at the edges, framed by oak and plane trees.

It makes an easy stop between Sarandë and Gjirokastër, and is one of the most photographed places in the country.

Don't miss in The Blue Eye

  • The viewing platform over the spring at first light
  • The short forest walk along the river from the car park
  • Combine with Gjirokastër or Sarandë in a day

How to get to The Blue Eye

  • From Sarandë: around 30–40 minutes by car.
  • By bus: Sarandë–Gjirokastër buses can drop you at the turn-off, then it's a walk of about 2 km.
  • Many Sarandë and Ksamil tours combine the Blue Eye with Gjirokastër.

Watch out for

  • Swimming and jumping into the spring are not allowed to protect it. Follow the signs.
  • The water is about 10 °C all year.
  • There's a small entrance and parking fee; bring cash in lek.
