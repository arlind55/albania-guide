Cities · The South-East

Korçë

Boulevards, beer and serenades in the south-east

Best time
May–October; August for the beer festival
Time to spend
1–2 days
Region
The South-East
Coordinates
40.62° N 20.78° E

Korçë sits on a high plateau at about 850 m, close to the borders with Greece and North Macedonia. It has a refined, slightly French air, with tree-lined boulevards, villas, a big Orthodox cathedral and a beautifully restored Ottoman bazaar.

It's known for Korça beer, brewed here since 1928, its summer beer festival, traditional serenade singing, and lakror, a savoury layered pie.

Don't miss in Korçë

  • The restored Old Bazaar and its cafés
  • The National Museum of Medieval Art and its icon collection
  • The Resurrection Cathedral
  • A Korça beer and a plate of lakror

How to get to Korçë

  • From Tirana: about 3.5–4 hours by bus or car.
  • From Pogradec and Lake Ohrid: about 45 minutes.

Watch out for

  • Winters are cold and snowy; pack accordingly from November to March.
  • Book ahead for the August beer festival.
