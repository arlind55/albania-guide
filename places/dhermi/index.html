Beaches · The South

Dhërmi

White pebbles, turquoise water and a hillside village

Best time
June and September; July–August for festivals and nightlife
Time to spend
2–3 days
Region
The South
Coordinates
40.15° N 19.64° E

Dhërmi is the first beach you reach coming down from the Llogara Pass: a long sweep of white pebbles and very clear water, with an old stone village and churches climbing the hill behind.

In high summer it's the Riviera's liveliest spot, with beach bars and music festivals. In June and September it's calm, warm and much cheaper.

Don't miss in Dhërmi

  • Swimming off the long main beach
  • Walking up through the old village and its churches
  • Gjipe beach and canyon, a hike or boat ride away
  • Sunset drinks on the beach

How to get to Dhërmi

  • From Vlorë: about 1–1.5 hours by car or bus over the Llogara Pass.
  • From Tirana: 3.5–4.5 hours by bus or car.
  • From Himarë: about 30 minutes north along the coast.

Watch out for

  • Prices and crowds peak in late July and August.
  • Beaches are pebble; bring water shoes.
  • Parking near the beach is tight in summer.
