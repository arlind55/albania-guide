Beaches · The South

Himarë

The Riviera's laid-back heart, with a castle on the hill

Best time
Late May to early October
Time to spend
2–4 days
Region
The South
Coordinates
40.10° N 19.74° E

Himarë is the most relaxed base on the Albanian Riviera: a small town strung along a bay, a choice of pebble beaches within walking distance, and an old hilltop village with views down the Ionian coast.

It's a great hub for beach-hopping. Livadhi and Potami are close by, while Gjipe, a beach at the mouth of a canyon, and the little fortress at Porto Palermo are short drives away.

Don't miss in Himarë

  • Sunset from Himarë Castle and the old village
  • Hike or take a boat to Gjipe beach and its canyon
  • Ali Pasha's fortress at Porto Palermo
  • Fresh fish at the harbour tavernas

How to get to Himarë

  • From Tirana: buses heading down the coast to Sarandë stop in Himarë; allow 4–5 hours.
  • By car from Vlorë: over the Llogara Pass, around 1.5–2 hours with stops. The views from the pass are among the best in the country.
  • From Sarandë: about 1.5 hours north by bus or car.

Watch out for

  • Beaches are pebbly. Water shoes make life much easier.
  • Coastal buses are frequent in summer but thin out sharply from October to May.
  • The Llogara road is steep with hairpins; take it slowly and use low gears on the way down.
