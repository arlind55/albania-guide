The classic first trip: the capital, the UNESCO towns of Berat and Gjirokastër, the Blue Eye spring and the best of the Ionian coast. It works by bus, but a rental car gives you more freedom on the coast.

Day 1 · Overnight in Tirana

Arrive in Tirana

Airport to centre: 25–40 minutes

Morning
Arrive at Tirana International Airport and head into the centre by airport bus or official taxi.
Afternoon
Skanderbeg Square, the National History Museum and Bunk'Art 2 give you the story of the country in an afternoon.
Evening
Dinner in Blloku.

Tip: If you're renting a car, pick it up on day 2 rather than driving in Tirana traffic straight off the plane.

Day 2 · Overnight in Berat

Tirana to Berat

About 2–2.5 hours by bus or car

Morning
Head south to Berat.
Afternoon
Settle into the old town, cross the Gorica bridge and walk the riverside.
Evening
Sunset over the Mangalem windows, then dinner with local wine.

Tip: Driving? Stop at a winery on the way in; the Berat area is Albania's best-known wine region.

Day 3 · Overnight in Gjirokastër

Berat to Gjirokastër

About 3–3.5 hours by car; longer by bus with a change

Morning
Berat Castle and the Onufri Museum before the day warms up.
Afternoon
Travel south to Gjirokastër through the Drino valley.
Evening
Wander the stone lanes of the old bazaar and eat in a traditional restaurant.

Tip: Buses stop in the new town. Take a short taxi up the hill to the old town with your bags.

Day 4 · Overnight in Ksamil

Gjirokastër to the coast via the Blue Eye

About 1.5–2 hours driving in total

Morning
Gjirokastër Castle, then a restored tower house such as Skenduli or Zekate.
Afternoon
Stop at the Blue Eye spring on the road to Sarandë, then continue to Ksamil.
Evening
First swim and a seafood dinner by the water.

Tip: By bus, take a Sarandë-bound service, get off at the Blue Eye turn-off and walk about 2 km in; then flag a later bus or taxi.

Day 5 · Overnight in Ksamil

Ksamil and Butrint

Butrint is about 10 minutes from Ksamil

Morning
Visit Butrint early, while it's cool and quiet: the Greek theatre, the baptistery and the Venetian tower.
Afternoon
Beach time, and swim or kayak out to the Ksamil islets.
Evening
Sunset drinks on Sarandë's promenade, looking across to Corfu.

Tip: In July and August, book your sunbeds and dinner tables early.

Day 6 · Overnight in Himarë

Up the Riviera to Himarë

About 1.5 hours by car or bus

Morning
Drive north along the coast, stopping at Porto Palermo's little fortress.
Afternoon
Beach time at Livadhi or Potami, or hike down to Gjipe beach and its canyon.
Evening
Climb to Himarë's old village for sunset, then fish by the harbour.

Tip: Coastal buses are frequent in summer but sparse from October to May. Check times locally.

Day 7 · Overnight in Tirana

Over the Llogara Pass to Tirana

About 4–5 hours by car or bus

Morning
A last swim, then up the Llogara Pass for the big view over the Riviera.
Afternoon
Continue via Vlorë back to Tirana, or straight to the airport.
Evening
Final dinner in Tirana.

Tip: The Llogara road is steep with hairpins. Take it slowly and use low gears on the way down.
