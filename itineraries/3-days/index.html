A short first taste that works with a long weekend and Tirana flights. You'll see the country's busy modern face, then slow down in one of its most beautiful old towns. No car needed.

Day 1 · Overnight in Tirana

Arrive in Tirana

Airport to centre: 25–40 minutes

Morning
Land at Tirana International Airport and take the airport bus or an official taxi into the centre. Drop your bags and get your bearings on Skanderbeg Square.
Afternoon
Spend a couple of hours at Bunk'Art 2, a Cold War bunker turned museum a short walk from the square. Then wander past the colourful buildings and the renovated Pyramid.
Evening
Dinner and drinks in Blloku, the former communist leaders' quarter, now the city's liveliest neighbourhood.

Tip: Get some lek from an ATM at the airport or in town; small cafés and taxis prefer cash.

Day 2 · Overnight in Berat

Tirana to Berat

Bus: about 2–2.5 hours

Morning
Ride the Dajti Ekspres cable car for a view over the city, or browse the New Bazaar for breakfast and coffee. Head to the bus terminal late morning.
Afternoon
Arrive in Berat and check into a guesthouse in the old Mangalem or Gorica quarter. Cross the Gorica footbridge and walk along the river.
Evening
Watch the evening light on the 'thousand windows' from the Gorica side, then have dinner with local wine.

Tip: Ask your Tirana accommodation which terminal Berat buses leave from that day.

Day 3 · Overnight in Tirana

Berat's castle, then back

Bus back to Tirana: about 2–2.5 hours

Morning
Climb to Berat Castle early, before the heat. People still live inside its walls, among Byzantine churches and the Onufri Museum of icons.
Afternoon
Lunch in the old town, then take an afternoon bus back to Tirana. If you have a flight, allow a good margin for traffic to the airport.
Evening
A last stroll along Tirana's Lana river and a final byrek or tavë kosi.

Tip: With an extra half day, a guided trip to the Osum Canyon or a family winery near Berat is well worth it.
