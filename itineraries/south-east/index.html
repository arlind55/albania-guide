A quieter side of Albania that most visitors miss: an ancient lake, an elegant highland city and a mountain village full of history. Good in spring, summer and early autumn, and very affordable.

Day 1 · Overnight in Pogradec

Tirana to Lake Ohrid

About 3 hours by bus or car

Morning
Leave Tirana heading east through the mountains.
Afternoon
Settle into Pogradec and walk the lakeside promenade.
Evening
Lake fish dinner by the water.

Tip: Sit on the right-hand side of the bus for lake views as you arrive.

Day 2 · Overnight in Korçë

Drilon springs, then on to Korçë

About 45 minutes to Korçë

Morning
Visit the clear springs and gardens at Drilon, and the lakeside village of Tushemisht.
Afternoon
Travel to Korçë and wander the restored Old Bazaar.
Evening
Korça beer and lakror pie in the bazaar.

Tip: Drilon is about 10 minutes from Pogradec by taxi.

Day 3 · Overnight in Korçë

Voskopojë day trip

About 30 minutes each way by car or taxi

Morning
Head up to Voskopojë and visit its frescoed churches.
Afternoon
Forest walk and a long lunch at a village guesthouse.
Evening
Back in Korçë, the National Museum of Medieval Art if you haven't seen it, then dinner.

Tip: Agree a return time with your taxi driver, or ask your guesthouse to call one for you.

Day 4 · Overnight in Tirana

Back to Tirana

About 3.5–4 hours by bus or car

Morning
Visit the Resurrection Cathedral and have a last coffee on the boulevard.
Afternoon
Travel back to Tirana.
Evening
Dinner in Blloku.

Tip: Morning buses are the most reliable; afternoon departures are fewer.
