The classic northern loop: a boat ride through a flooded gorge, two high valleys and one of the best day hikes in the Balkans. Best from June to September, when the pass is clear of snow. No car needed; minibuses and guesthouses handle the logistics.

Day 1 · Overnight in Shkodër

Arrive in Shkodër

From Tirana: about 2 hours by bus

Morning
Travel north from Tirana, or cross from Montenegro.
Afternoon
Walk the pedestrian old town and visit the Marubi photography museum.
Evening
Sunset from Rozafa Castle over three rivers and the lake.

Tip: Ask your guesthouse to book the next morning's Koman ferry transfer and to arrange luggage transport to Theth for day 3.

Day 2 · Overnight in Valbona

The Lake Koman ferry to Valbona

About 2 hours by minibus, 2.5–3 hours by boat, then 1 hour to Valbona

Morning
Early pick-up in Shkodër and the drive to the Koman dam.
Afternoon
The ferry through the gorge to Fierzë, then a minibus up to Valbona.
Evening
Dinner at your guesthouse and an early night before the hike.

Tip: Sit on the open deck for the best views, and bring snacks; there's little food on board.

Day 3 · Overnight in Theth

Hike Valbona to Theth

On foot: about 6–8 hours over the Valbona Pass

Morning
Set off early and climb through beech forest to the Valbona Pass, at around 1,800 m.
Afternoon
Descend into the Theth valley, with a stop at the café huts on the way down.
Evening
Arrive in Theth to a hearty guesthouse dinner. You've earned it.

Tip: Carry at least 2 litres of water, sun protection and a layer. Snow can linger on the pass into June.

Day 4 · Overnight in Theth

A day in Theth

On foot around the valley

Morning
Walk to the Grunas waterfall and the Lock-in Tower.
Afternoon
Hike to Theth's Blue Eye pool for a (very cold) swim; allow most of the afternoon for the round trip.
Evening
Sunset by Theth's church and another home-cooked dinner.

Tip: There are no ATMs in Theth. Make sure you brought enough cash in lek from Shkodër.

Day 5 · Overnight in Shkodër

Back to Shkodër

About 2.5–3 hours by minibus

Morning
Take the morning minibus down the newly paved road to Shkodër.
Afternoon
Cycle to Lake Shkodër or the Mesi Bridge, or continue to Tirana.
Evening
Celebrate with dinner on Kole Idromeno street.

Tip: Minibuses out of Theth fill up; reserve your seat through your guesthouse the day before.
