Before you go

Three things first-timers wish they'd known

Buses run on local knowledge

There's no national timetable. Your guesthouse is the best source for departure times and stops, and most buses leave in the morning.

Cash rules in the mountains

Carry Albanian lek. There are no ATMs in Theth or Valbona, and minibuses and guesthouses rarely take cards.

Roads take longer than maps say

Mountain and coastal roads are slow and winding. Add a third to your map's estimate and avoid driving them after dark.
